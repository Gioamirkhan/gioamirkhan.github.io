Excel Best and Worst Practices

Most people learn Excel on the job — and develop a quiet collection of worst practices: files only the creator understands, formulas avoided in favour of typing, and a powerful tool used at a fraction of its potential.

This workshop builds the foundations: the functions worth knowing, practices that keep files consistent, and habits that make your team's spreadsheets compatible with each other.

Excel to Power BI

Power BI is what happens when Excel's analytical power meets proper data visualisation. For anyone comfortable with Excel functions, the jump to Power BI is smaller than you think — and the results are incomparably more powerful. In this workshop, learn how to get set up for that transition and where to go from there.
